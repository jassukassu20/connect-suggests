Why it's different

  • Works without history. 4 in 10 businesses have nothing to copy. Connect reads how customers behaved on last year's holidays (on secretly closed days, clicks halve) and what similar businesses do.
  • Learns every season. An edit becomes next year's answer, a “no” is remembered, and repeat approvals unlock “handle holidays for me”.
  • Asks when unsure instead of guessing.

Safe by design

  • Nothing goes live without the owner's OK or standing permission.
  • Stricter rule before suggesting “closed”, because that mistake hurts most.
  • Businesses that look closed for good go to a person, never an automatic message.
  • One bundled message per season, in the owner's language, channel and preferred time. Opt-out is respected.

Same loop, more jobs

  • Outdated regular hours spotted in reviews
  • Seasonal breaks (summer terraces, ski rentals)
  • Review replies and automatic posts: “Open late on arena night”

Ready for Connect

  • Writes hours through the same API Connect already runs on (Yext)
  • Finnish, Swedish, Norwegian and English messages
  • Full audit trail: every change, who approved it, and why
